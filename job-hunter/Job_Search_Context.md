### Title List (used by both passes)

A matching title is any combination of a **Seniority Prefix** + **Functional Root** below. Treat all punctuation and word-order variants as equivalent — "VP of Revenue Operations" ≈ "VP, Revenue Operations" ≈ "Vice President, Revenue Operations" ≈ "Revenue Operations, VP".

**Seniority Prefixes** (any of these qualify):
- VP / Vice President
- Head of
- Senior Director / Sr. Director / Senior Director of
- Director / Director of

(At large enterprise orgs — Samsara, Stripe, ServiceNow, Oracle, Workday, SAP — a Director or Senior Director title is often the VP-equivalent altitude. Include them.)

**Functional Roots** (any of these qualify):
- Revenue Operations
- GTM Operations / Go-To-Market Operations
- Sales Operations
- Commercial Operations
- Business Operations (only when GTM/revenue-scoped — skip if it's finance or product ops)
- Sales Strategy & Operations / GTM Strategy & Operations / Revenue Strategy & Operations
- GTM Systems / Revenue Systems
- Partner Operations / Channel Operations (given ServiceNow + WalkMe background)

**Adjacent titles to flag separately** (not a direct match, but worth a look):
- Chief of Staff, Revenue / GTM / CRO Org
- Any "Head of" or "VP" role inside a CRO/CCO org with a strategy + operations scope
